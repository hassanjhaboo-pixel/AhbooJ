# Kafneo new iced lines: gang sheet prompts (free-tier version)

Six images total, plus one optional. Each sheet packs several assets into one image on a flat background,
and I cut them apart. Free tiers allow only a few images a day, so this is the whole job.

## Rules for every sheet
- **Paste the STYLE BLOCK first**, then the sheet prompt.
- **Generate sheet 1 first.** Then attach it as a reference to sheets 2 and 3 and say "same cup, same lighting as the attached image".
- **Ask for the biggest size offered.** If there's an aspect ratio option, use the one listed for that sheet.
- **Save as PNG** if you can, otherwise the highest-quality download. Don't screenshot, because it lowers the resolution.
- **Replace the drink descriptions** with your real recipes before you generate.
- **Name the files** `sheet1.png` to `sheet6.png` and send them together.

Either Gemini or ChatGPT works for all of them. If you have to split, use Gemini for 1 to 4 and ChatGPT for 5 and 6.

## STYLE BLOCK (paste first, every time)

> Premium commercial beverage photography for a specialty coffee shop. Photorealistic, 85mm lens, one soft warm key light from the upper left. Crisp detail, real condensation droplets. Every cup is the same clear 16 oz plastic iced-coffee cup with a flat clear lid and a straight paper straw, no logo, no label, no sleeve. No hands, no people, no text, no watermark.

## Optional sheet 7: macro close-ups (square 1:1)

> Four extreme macro close-ups in a 2 × 2 grid with a thin gap between them: condensation droplets running down a clear iced-coffee cup; pumpkin cold foam settling over ice; whipped cream with cinnamon dust; ivory coconut foam with toasted coconut flakes. Warm light, shallow depth of field.

## What I build from these
- **Cups:** cut from sheets 1 and 2 (front hero) and sheet 3 (the overhead dive shots).
- **Floating life:** the 12 ingredients from sheet 4 drift at three depths, with the near ones blurred past the lens.
- **Worlds:** sheets 5 and 6 are split into a far layer and a table layer, so the camera can move with real parallax and focus pulls.
- **Low hero angles:** a camera move from table height on the front cups.
- **Close-ups:** sheet 7 if you make it. If not, I punch in on the cups.

## Quick checks before sending
- All three cups on each sheet look like the same cup, the same size, in one row.
- Nothing overlaps or touches the edge of the image.
- No text, logos or hands anywhere.
