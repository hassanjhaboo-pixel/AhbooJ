## Confessional shot (reality-TV cutaway) — reusable pattern
```
SET:      character's colour wall (blurred), a brand poster, string lights, pool of light
CAMERA:   medium close-up, handheld 0.6, slow push 1.00→1.04; punch-in (crash zoom) on the punchline
HUD:      REC · CONFESSIONAL (top-left) · day/time stamp (top-right) · name + funny tag lower third
LINE:     2–3 short parts; cut to a tighter angle on the last part
GRADE:    vignette + grain; tint by mood (warm = sweet, purple = scheming, sepia = old/dusty)
SOUND:    camera-shutter click on the cut-in; sting / impact on the punchline
```
