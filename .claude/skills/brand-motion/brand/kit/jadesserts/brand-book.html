A little box of sweet surprises

For people who show love with food, Jadesserts is the home bakery whose curated sweet boxes turn an ordinary day into an occasion.

Strategy

Make every sweet moment feel like a gift: small-batch treats, packed with care into a box worth opening.

Vision

The first name people think of when they want to surprise someone with something sweet.

Brand promise

A little box of sweet surprises, worth every wait.

Values

  • Made with love, made in small batches
  • Delight in the details
  • Generous, never fussy
  • Worth the wait

Audience

  • The gifter: a thoughtful, pretty present that arrives ready to wow
  • The treat-yourselfer: a weekly reward that feels special
  • The party planner: boxes for birthdays, office treats and events

Personality

cutesywhimsicalaffectionategenerous

We are not: luxury-snobby, sarcastic, clinical.

Voice & tone

We sound cutesy, whimsical, affectionate. On-screen copy: max 6 words per card, sentence case. Default CTA: “Order your sweet box”.

Sweet, not sugary

Warm and affectionate, with a wink.

Not: Baby talk or endless exclamation marks.

Playful

Small jokes, food puns, reality-TV drama about dessert.

Not: Jokes at the customer’s expense.

Generous

Lead with what’s inside the box and why it’s a treat.

Not: Discount-shouting or scarcity pressure.

Say

Avoid

  • basic

Shape & motif

Language

circles · radius 38px@1080 · stroke 6px

Signature motif

heart bullets + pastel confetti (circles, sparkles, hearts); a 4-segment pastel bar that stands for the 4 items in the box

Iconography

Filled, rounded shapes: hearts, cherries, sparkles, scalloped badges. No thin-line icons.

Imagery

Bright, soft, high-key food photography on cream or pastel backgrounds; treats cut out cleanly and shown whole, never cropped mid-bite. Illustrations are rounded, pastel and toy-like.

Do

  • Shoot from slightly above with soft daylight
  • Show the box open with the treats arranged
  • Use pastel props: ribbons, hearts, confetti

Don't

  • Dark, moody lighting
  • Cluttered tables or busy patterns
  • Watermarked stock images

Do & don't

Do

  • Warm cream is the default background; stage colours are pastels (pink, lavender, mint, peach)
  • Use heart glyphs as the list bullet and confetti shapes for texture
  • Everything rounded; soft, bouncy motion (overshoot) rather than mechanical
  • Price is its own oversized callout

Don't

  • No stark white or grey backgrounds, no black shadows (tint shadows plum/pink)
  • Never put white small text on the coral primary (3.1:1) — display sizes only
  • No more than one saturated coral block per scene
