Before you hit record

Hold the phone vertical. Every shot.

4K at 60fps if your phone has it (Settings → Camera → Record Video).

Wipe the lens with your shirt or a microfiber.

Tap and hold on the screen to lock focus before each shot.

Hold still 5 to 10 seconds per shot. Brace your elbows.

No zooming while recording. Walk closer instead.

Shade or garage beats hard sun. Interior lights on inside.

Film it 3 ways: far, medium, and very close.

Rules

No licence plates in shot. Film around them or cover them.

Ask first before filming a customer's car, house or face.

Sound on. Leave the noise in (washer, foam, vacuum). It gets mixed under the music.
