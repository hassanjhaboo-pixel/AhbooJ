# AREA 47 mascot (v1.1, working name "CLASSIFIED")

An alien "head of security" with street energy: durag (antennae poke through), heavy-lidded unbothered eyes, iced grill,
chunky iced chain with a UFO pendant, black hoodie with the 47 patch, a microfiber towel on the shoulder (he's a detailer),
arms crossed, and a small "47" under the eye. Original character; the energy is inspired by street/rap culture, with no real
person's likeness, tattoos or weapons.

- Layers: `brand/assets/area47/mascot/` (head, body, eyes-{cool,wink,side,hype,closed}, mouth-{smirk,grin,ayy}), all 400×490 head space
- Kit: `kits/area47-mascot.mjs` → `mascot({ x, y, w, faces: [[t, eyes, mouth], …], nods: [t, …] })`
- `make.mjs` → `intro.json` (4:5 beam-down intro: lands, nods on the beat, blinks, side-eye, wink + grill sparkle) and `sheet.json` (4 expressions)
